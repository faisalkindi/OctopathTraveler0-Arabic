### 雷公烈風 ###
id=94666 EN=Thundersquall BEFORE=رعد إله الريح العاتية AFTER=الرعد الإلهي والريح العاتية REASON=The original Arabic `رعد إله الريح العاتية` implies 'thunder of the god of fierce wind', which misinterprets `雷公` (Thunder God) as 'god of thunder' and then combines it with 'fierce wind' in a possessive structure. The Japanese `雷公烈風` is more like 'Thunder God and Fierce Wind' or 'Thunder God's Fierce Wind'. `الرعد الإلهي والريح العاتية` (Divine Thunder and the Fierce Wind) better captures both elements without the awkward possessive chain.
id=96155 EN=Thundersquall BEFORE=ريح رعد إله الرعد AFTER=عاصفة رعدية REASON=عاصفة رعدية (thunderstorm/thundersquall) is a more concise and natural translation of Thundersquall than ريح رعد إله الرعد, which is redundant and clunky.
