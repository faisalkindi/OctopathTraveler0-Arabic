Octopath Traveler 0, a Square Enix HD-2D JRPG set in the world of Orsterra. The protagonist is a player-created, silent, gender-customizable character from the village of Wishvale, which is burned down by three rival warlords early in the story; the protagonist sets out on a journey of revenge and rebirth. This scene is from the "Bestower of Wealth" storyline, centered on Lady Herminia ("the Covetous Witch"), a corrupt noblewoman who controls Valore through a drug ("the powder") monopoly. Bargello leads a small mafia-style gang (crew: Ludo, Fra, Tiziano, Pierro Della, Rosso) planning to infiltrate Herminia's mansion.

Register: warm, natural, everyday Modern Standard Arabic throughout -- NOT archaic/classical MSA, NOT regional dialect. Herminia and her household speak in a more refined/courtly register (using ...わ /...わよ sentence-final markers in the Japanese, signaling elevated feminine speech); Bargello's gang speaks in a rougher, blunt, informal register -- achieve this distinction entirely within MSA via word choice and sentence structure, never by switching to real-world dialect.

The player-character (protagonist) is addressed by NPCs constantly but has NO fixed gender in-game (no gender-branching dialogue exists) -- any line whose addressee is "player" must use gender-neutral Arabic phrasing.

Diacritics (تشكيل/harakat): leave almost every line completely bare (no diacritics at all) -- only about 1 in 10 lines should carry any mark. When you do add one: shadda (ّ) is the priority, use it whenever gemination is meaning-bearing; a single fatha/kasra short vowel only where a word would otherwise be genuinely misread. Never fully vocalize a whole sentence.
